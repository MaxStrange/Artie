# Research Roadmap

This contribution guide is for people who want to do significant work towards extending our understanding
of human cognitive development or the field of robotics. This guide shows the most up-to-date plan for
research publications and conference presentations with regards to the Artie Project.

We are currently looking for helpful contributors to join the "Lab" (it's just us, working on this in our
own time, in a remote/distributed fashion). If you think you can be a significant help towards:

* Reviewing literature
* Editing papers/documentation
* Implementing features
* Designing experiments
* Doing data analysis

then please, get in touch!
